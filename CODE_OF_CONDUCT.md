# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@jacksontae](https://github.com/jacksontae).
Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions,
and may block anyone who violates this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
